# World Guide — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 24, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what World Guide ("we", "us", the "App") collects, why, and your
choices. This document is a starting point provided for convenience and is **not legal advice** —
have it reviewed by a qualified attorney before publishing.

## Who we are
World Guide is an iOS application that gives AI-generated audio tour guides for places around you.
Contact: colebui@gmail.com.

## Information we collect

**Location.** With your permission, we use your device's location to show nearby places and to
provide directions. Location is processed to fetch nearby points of interest and is not used for
advertising. You can revoke location access anytime in iOS Settings.

**Account information.** The App works anonymously by default. If you choose to create an account,
we collect the identifier from your sign-in method — your email address (email sign-in) or the
Apple-provided identifier/relay email (Sign in with Apple). You may use Apple's "Hide My Email."

**Saved content.** Lists of places you save are stored in your account so they sync across your
devices.

**Usage data.** To operate the free tier and prevent abuse, we record limited usage counters tied
to your account (e.g., which days you used the App and request counts). We do not sell this data.

**Subscription status.** If you subscribe, we and our payment processors record your subscription
state (active/expired) to unlock features. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your card
details.

**Diagnostics.** We may collect crash and performance diagnostics to improve reliability.

## How your information is used
- Provide nearby places, guides, audio narration, and directions.
- Maintain your account and sync your saved lists.
- Enforce free-tier limits and protect against abuse.
- Manage subscriptions and entitlements.
- Maintain and improve the App.

## Service providers (processors)
We share data with providers only as needed to run the App:
- **Google / Firebase** — authentication, database, hosting, and Places/Maps data.
- **OpenAI** — generates guide text from place information.
- **ElevenLabs** — generates audio narration.
- **RevenueCat** — manages subscription state.
- **Apple** — payment processing and Sign in with Apple.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Data retention
We keep your account data until you delete your account. Generated guide content about public places
is cached to reduce cost and may be retained. Place data obtained from Google is not retained beyond
the limits permitted by Google's terms.

## Your rights and choices
- **Access/portability and deletion.** You can delete your account and all associated data in-app via
  **Account → Delete account**. This removes your saved lists, usage counters, and subscription record.
- **Location.** Revoke anytime in iOS Settings.
- **GDPR (EEA/UK)** and **CCPA/CPRA (California)**: you have rights to access, correct, delete, and
  restrict processing of your personal data. Contact us at the email above to exercise them.

## Children
World Guide is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes
We'll update this policy as the App evolves and revise the "Last updated" date above.
